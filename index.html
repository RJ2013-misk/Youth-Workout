Rugby & Track Workout

Shoulders • Back • Chest — Machine Only • No Overhead/Spine Compression

Gym Checklist

    Guidelines

    • Use light–moderate weight with perfect form; stop 2–3 reps before failure.
    • No overhead pressing or vertical spine loading.
    • Slow lowers (2–3 seconds) and smooth control on every rep.
    • Warm up 5–8 minutes and finish with easy cardio and mobility.
    • Ask a coach/parent to supervise machine setup and form.

    This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice. If anything hurts (sharp/pinching pain), stop and tell a coach/parent.
